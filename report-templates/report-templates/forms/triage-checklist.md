# Triage Checklist - Who Needs Scan First?

Use this at registration desk. Tick and decide priority.

### RED - Urgent - Scan First
- [ ] Pregnant woman with bleeding / severe pain
- [ ] Child with high fever + breathing difficulty
- [ ] Accident / injury / broken bone suspected
- [ ] Severe abdominal pain

### YELLOW - Same Day
- [ ] Pregnant woman for routine check (2nd / 3rd trimester)
- [ ] Long time cough, weight loss
- [ ] Joint pain / back pain for months
- [ ] Gallbladder / kidney pain history

### GREEN - Can Wait / Counselling Only
- [ ] General weakness, wants full body check
- [ ] Old reports review only

### Registration Details to Note
Name, Age, Village, Phone, Symptoms, BP, Sugar if diabetic

**Rule:** No patient goes home without counselling, even if no scan needed.
