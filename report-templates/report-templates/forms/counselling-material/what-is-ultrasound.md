# What is Ultrasound? - Simple Explanation for Patients

### In Simple Urdu / Punjabi

Ultrasound ek safe test hai. Is mein koi dard nahi hota, koi radiation nahi hoti.

Machine se awaz ki lehren jati hain aur screen par andar ki tasveer banti hai.

### Pregnant Women - Kya Hoga?
- Pet par thanda gel lagayenge
- 10-15 minute test hoga
- Bachay ka dil, growth, pani check karenge
- Aap screen par bacha dekh sakti hain

### Common Fears - Ghalat Fehmiyan
- "Ultrasound se bacha kharab hota hai?" - NAHI, ye bilkul safe hai.
- "X-Ray se cancer hota hai?" - 1 X-Ray se kuch nahi hota. Ham lead apron use karte hain. Pregnant aurat ka X-Ray nahi karte jab tak bohat zaroori na ho.
- "Report mein sab kuch aa jayega?" - Ultrasound sirf tasveer deta hai, doctor ko mil kar faisla karna hota hai.

### After Scan - Kya Karein?
- Report ko sambhal kar rakhein
- Agar referral slip di hai, to zaroor hospital jayein
- Koi dawai apni marzi se na lein
