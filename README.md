# Sehat on Wheels
### Portable Ultrasound & X-Ray for Villages Who Cannot Travel

> If the patient cannot come to the hospital, the hospital will go to the patient.

Sehat on Wheels is an open-source toolkit for running a one-day portable ultrasound and X-ray camp in a rural village.

Most villages in Punjab, Pakistan have 0 imaging. Patients cannot travel to Lahore due to cost, transport, and fear. This project gives any clinic, radiographer, or student a free, ready-to-use system.

### What is inside?
- Ultrasound & X-ray report templates (English + Urdu)
- Triage checklist - who needs scan first?
- Counselling material in simple Urdu/Punjabi
- Consent form, registration sheet, referral slip
- Camp planning guide - cost, team, equipment

### Who can use it?
Radiographers, Sonologists, Medical Imaging Students, Rural Clinics, NGOs

### How to use it?
1. Download the forms
2. Print them for your camp
3. Use the report templates for same-day reporting
4. Give referral slip in Urdu to patient

### Camp Model
- Cost: Rs. 25,000 - 35,000 per camp
- Goal: 150 patients per camp
- Team: Sonologist, X-ray Tech, Counsellor, Driver + Helper
- Reports: Ultrasound in 30 mins, X-ray in 2 hours
